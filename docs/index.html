    Only Zewa Deluxe toilet paper is tracked (all variants, any pack size). Zewa Everyday and other lines are left out. The roll count comes from the product name; the price used is the lowest of the regular, campaign and loyalty card price.

    Loyalty prices need the store's card or account: Selver Partnerkaart, Barbora AITÄH, Bauhof logged-in customer price. Coop has no central web shop: "Coop" is the Coop Haapsalu e-shop, and "Coop Lasnamäe (Wolt)" is the Wolt delivery price, which includes Wolt's markup.

    A small personal project, not affiliated with Zewa or any store. Source on GitHub.
