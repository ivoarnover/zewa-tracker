Store problems

    Only Zewa Deluxe toilet paper is tracked (all variants, any pack size). Zewa Everyday and other lines are left out. The roll count comes from the product name; the price used is the lowest of the regular, campaign and loyalty price.

    Loyalty prices need the store's card or account: Selver Partnerkaart, Barbora AITÄH card, Bauhof customer account. Coop has no central web shop, so Coop prices come from the Coop Haapsalu e-shop and, marked as Wolt, from Coop Lasnamäe on Wolt.

    A small personal project, not affiliated with Zewa or any store. Source on GitHub.
